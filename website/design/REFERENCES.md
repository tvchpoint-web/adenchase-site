## Acceptance criteria carried by these references

**Living Circuit**
- The board is recognisably a genuine photographed circuit board, never vector, cartoon or "sci-fi" circuitry.
- It is mostly concealed in Midnight Navy and revealed only near the signal, with a soft falloff and no hard circular edge.
- Metal glints appear only near the light, and darkness returns as the signal moves on.
- There is no visible tiling or seams.
- The signal route never overlaps text, buttons, navigation or essential photography.
- The route travels horizontally and vertically through open space, is synchronised with scroll, and reverses cleanly.

**Opening**
- Ambient blue light, then ADEN-CHASE, then the underline lit left to right into the green block, then cyan and sapphire in turn, then STRATEGIES and the descriptor.
- Overlays only; the authentic logo is never redrawn.
- There is no navy gap between the underline and the green block at any size.

**Photography**
- Premium, graded into the palette, with no prominent faces. The cargo photograph stays in Market Growth.

**AI Engineering demos**
- Elegant and credible, labelled "Illustrative". No hub-and-spoke "AI" box.
